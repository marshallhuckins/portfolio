About Me

My technical skills include HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Angular, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, Python, Flutter, and WordPress. I'm committed to writing clean, maintainable code and designing accessible digital experiences that serve a wide range of users.


I'm currently pursuing my Bachelor of Science in Web Development at Bellevue University, building on an Associate’s degree in Front-End Web Development. I've completed projects involving dynamic data-driven apps, content management systems, and mobile-first responsive design.


Outside of coding, I love exploring new tech tools, managing Airbnb listings, working on freelance projects, and dreaming of working remotely from Puerto Vallarta, Mexico. I'm always eager to collaborate on meaningful projects and continue growing as a developer!


My journey into tech started with a deep curiosity about how websites and applications are built. What began as a hobby soon turned into a passion, leading me to pursue formal education and hands-on projects. I’m driven by the creativity and problem-solving that development offers, and I’m excited to keep growing in this ever-evolving field.
